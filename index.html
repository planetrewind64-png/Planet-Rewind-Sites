PLANET REWIND

Earth, played backwards.

Planet Rewind is a YouTube channel that turns open satellite imagery into short timelapses of how places on Earth change over time: new cities and airports, shifting rivers, volcanic eruptions, shrinking lakes and glaciers.

Videos are made with a small in-house tool that renders imagery from the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem and uploads the finished videos to the Planet Rewind channel through the YouTube Data API. The tool is used only by the channel owner, for the channel owner's own account.
