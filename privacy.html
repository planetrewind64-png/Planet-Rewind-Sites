Privacy Policy

Planet Rewind upload tool. Last updated: YOUR-DATE

This policy covers the Planet Rewind upload tool, a private script run by the owner of the Planet Rewind YouTube channel.

Who uses it

The tool is used only by the channel owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

What it accesses

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the signed-in account and to read view counts of those videos. It does not read, collect or store viewer data, comments, subscriber information or any other personal data.

What it stores

The tool stores a sign-in token so the owner does not have to sign in for every upload, plus a list of the videos it has made and uploaded. Nothing is sent anywhere except Google's YouTube API, the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (for satellite imagery) and a private Telegram chat that notifies the owner.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. The YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
